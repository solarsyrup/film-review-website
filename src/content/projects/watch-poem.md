# Watch

i keep my watch on me
or near me
to remind me of the time

the time that flows through me
and with me
the time that has already happened
and is happening and will ever happen

its gentle ticks remind me of
time that i will not earn back
though i will bask in it for eternity

the time that i was air in a thousand-year old tree
the time i was food my mother ingested
the time i was late to a soccer game
the time i will be in the ground, reclaimed by nature
the time i will be dust in the cosmos
