I find myself on the beach on the Atlantic coast

I sit down and take my sandals off

I feel the sand in between my toes

I pick up two handfuls of sand, one on either side

The left hand sand is rough and clumps together

Good sand to build a castle or something that lasts

The right hand sand is smooth and its grains move individually 

As my grip loosens its grains blow with the wind

A girl my age walks past and probably wonders why the hell i'm examining two handfuls of sand and promptly turns around and walks the other way

I pick up my towel 

And toss it over my left shoulder with my right hand
