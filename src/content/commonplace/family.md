I think it’s a seriously neglected and overlooked fact that everybody and every single living thing is related.

Everybody is apart of one big family.

The trees bending in the wind, a mushroom wrapping around it, a rolly poly on the other side of the world, me, and you are all related. Earth and its inhabitants are all descended from one common ancestor. 

Every kind and cruel act ever performed, whether performed out of necessity or malice were performed upon a member of our family. 

This big family of ours is all the life we know of for now; and until we find out otherwise, all life is one in the same.
