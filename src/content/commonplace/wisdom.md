My family goes camping to various state parks in Ohio every year in cabins with a large group of family friends. This past summer, standing at the front of one of my best friend's family's cabin, I knock on the door. My friend's name is Aidan, and his dad is named Jim. I knock on the door, to see my friend for the first time in seven odd months. His dad answers, and we chat for a while. At a certain point, he starts giving me advice. I don't remember exactly what he said, but this is the important bits. 

> "I know you've always been a smart, hard-working kid. Keep your hands clean and your head up kid. Whatever you go through, you've got people on the other side waiting for ya. Keep on keepin' on bud."

*-Jim Langdon*

I don't know what it was but it stuck with me. It's a joke between that friend and I now; whenever his dad is brought up, one of us will usually go: "keep your head up and your hands clean, kid," followed by our shared confusion on why he told me this.
